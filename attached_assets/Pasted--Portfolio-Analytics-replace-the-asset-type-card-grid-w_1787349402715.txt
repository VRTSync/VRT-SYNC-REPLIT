# Portfolio Analytics — replace the asset-type card grid with four layer cards

*Reference mockup: `mockups/analytics-kpi-rethink.html`, **option A**. Awaiting a number.*

## What & Why

The current Tier 1 renders **one card per asset type** — eleven boxes on the PNC org, arranged in four bands. Three of those bands end in dead space, Trees spends a full row on a single number, and every figure appears again as a column in the Locations table below.

Two earlier passes made the cards smaller and the bands horizontal. Both helped and neither fixed it, because the problem is the **granularity**, not the sizing: an eleven-item inventory breakdown is not a KPI row.

**Replace it with four layer cards** — one per layer, equal width, single row. Roughly **145px** against ~450px today, which puts the Locations table on the first screen.

Nothing is lost. Every per-asset-type figure remains in the Locations table, in full.

## Layout

### Row 1 — four portfolio KPIs (compact)

| Card | Value | Subline |
|---|---|---|
| Locations | `10` | `49 assets each` |
| Total Assets | `492` | `4 layers` |
| **Managed Area** | `426,564 ft²` | `9.8 acres · 148 areas` |
| Spend YTD | `$52,696` | `109 services · $483 each` |

**Managed Area is new** — the sum of every area-type `sqFt` across the selection. It is a number a client asks for directly, and it replaces a slot that previously held an asset-type count.

**Services YTD folds into the Spend subline.** If it should stay its own card, drop Managed Area and say so — but not both; four is the limit before this row becomes the problem it is replacing.

Target height **≈ 62px**: value ~19px, label ~9.5px, subline ~10px, padding `9px 16px`.

### Row 2 — four layer cards

`repeat(auto-fit, minmax(200px, 1fr))` — **not a fixed 4 columns.** Layers with zero assets in the current selection are not rendered, so an org with two active layers gets two full-width cards rather than two cards and a gap. This is what guarantees no ragged edge at any filter or viewport.

Each card:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ IRRIGATION                32% of assets │   ← name left, share right
│ 158                                     │   ← layer total, in layer colour
│ 132 zones · 10 controllers · 9 backflow · +3 │  ← top types, truncated
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
   ↑ 3px left border in the layer colour
```

Target height **≈ 68px**.

### What the layer total is

**Area layers show area. Count layers show count.**

| Layer | Headline | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Landscape | `248,197 ft²` | its types carry `sqFt` |
| Snow | `178,367 ft²` | same |
| Irrigation | `158` | points — no area |
| Trees | `186` | points — no area |

A layer counts as an area layer when **any** of its active types has `sqFt` data. Mixed layers lead with area and mention the count in the subline.

## ⚠️ The share percentage needs its denominator stated — my mockup got this wrong

The mockup shows bare percentages: Irrigation 32%, Landscape 58%, Trees 38%, Snow 42%. **Those do not sum to 100 and appear to be nonsense.**

They are not nonsense — they use two different denominators — but nothing on the card says so, which is worse than omitting them.

**Two families, each internally consistent:**

| Family | Denominator | Members |
|---|---|---|
| Assets | 492 total assets | Irrigation 158 = **32%**, Trees 186 = **38%**, area-type polygons 148 = 30% |
| Area | 426,564 ft² | Landscape 248,197 = **58%**, Snow 178,367 = **42%** |

Each family sums to 100%. **Label the denominator on every card** — `32% of assets`, `58% of area`. Without it, a reader adding 32 + 58 + 38 + 42 gets 170 and stops trusting the page.

If that reads as clutter, **drop the percentage entirely.** An unexplained number is worse than no number.

### Subline truncation

List types by descending count, `·` separated, truncated to fit one line with `+N` for the remainder — `132 zones · 10 controllers · 9 backflow · +3`.

Abbreviate large figures here only (`194K bluegrass`). **The Managed Area KPI keeps its full separated number** — the whole point of the earlier area-first change was that square footage is legible.

Never wrap to a second line; the fixed card height is what keeps the row even.

## Behaviour that must survive

- **Layer focus chips** still filter which layer cards render — selecting Irrigation shows one card, full width.
- **Group filters** still drive every number.
- **Zero-count layers stay hidden.**
- **Coverage caveat.** Where `sqFt` is missing on some polygons, the Managed Area KPI and any area-layer card must still show it — `based on 34 of 41 areas` or similar. This is the only place it now surfaces, so it cannot be dropped in the simplification.

## What must NOT change

- **Any number.** Layout only. Totals, ratios, Tier 2, Tier 3 and CSV export are untouched.
- **The CSV export** — still exports the Locations table in full.
- **One network request.**
- **Layer colours** — `MAP_LAYER_DISPLAY`, already correct after the last pass.
- **Tier 2 and Tier 3.**

## How to verify

- [ ] **Locations table header visible without scrolling at 1920×1080.** The acceptance test.
- [ ] Tier 1 is two rows: four KPI cards, four layer cards. No band labels, no per-asset-type cards.
- [ ] Every percentage states its denominator; the asset family sums to 100% and the area family sums to 100%.
- [ ] Managed Area equals the sum of every area type — **426,564 ft²** on the full PNC selection.
- [ ] Total Assets still **492**; Irrigation 158 + Trees 186 + 148 area polygons = 492.
- [ ] Filter to one layer → one full-width card, no gap.
- [ ] Filter to a group with two active layers → two full-width cards, no gap.
- [ ] Clear one asset's `sqFt` → the coverage caveat appears on both the Managed Area KPI and its layer card.
- [ ] Sublines never wrap; long type lists truncate with `+N`.
- [ ] Every per-asset-type figure is still available in the Locations table.
- [ ] CSV export byte-identical.

## Out of scope

- Making the layer cards clickable or filterable.
- Tier 3 changes.
- Any new metric beyond Managed Area.
