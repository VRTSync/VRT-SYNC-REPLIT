# Portfolio Analytics — compact KPI cards, distinguishable layer palette

*Follow-on to #546. Awaiting a number. Based on a screenshot of the live page at 2600px wide.*

## What & Why

#546 landed the layer colour and area-first figures correctly. Two things it introduced or exposed:

**1. The cards eat the page.** Nine cards consume roughly 700px of vertical space before the Locations table starts, so the table — the densest, most useful thing on the page — sits below the fold on a 1440px-tall screen.

**2. Two of the four layer colours are near-duplicates.** Landscape `#2E8B57` against Trees `#006400` are both dark greens; Irrigation `#3498db` against Snow `#4A90E2` are both mid-blues. Side by side on a card grid they are not tellable apart.

There is also **a third problem visible in the screenshot that neither of us specced**: because each layer band starts a new grid row, four Irrigation cards fill one row and the rest of the width goes empty. At 2600px the right half of the screen is blank above the table while the content is pushed down.

## 1 · Make the bands horizontal, not stacked

This is the biggest win and it is a layout change, not a sizing one.

Today each band is `label above → cards below`, so every band forces a row break and wastes the remaining width.

**Give each band a left-hand label column and let its cards flow across the full width:**

```
IRRIGATION   [Backflow 3] [Controller 3] [Zone 43] [Quick Connect 1]
LANDSCAPE    [Bluegrass 25,687 ft²] [Landscape Bed 21,277 ft²]
TREES        [Tree 42]
SNOW         [Truck Plow 34,250 ft²] [Hand Shovel 3,184 ft²]
```

A two-column grid per band — fixed ~110px label column, cards in the remainder — turns four wrapped rows plus four heading rows into four dense rows. On a wide screen that alone roughly halves the vertical footprint.

Below ~900px, fall back to the current stacked arrangement.

## 2 · Shrink the cards

Current values and targets:

| Property | Now | Target |
|---|---|---|
| `.pa-card` padding | `14px 18px` | `10px 14px` |
| `.pa-card-value` font-size | `24px` | `19px` |
| `.pa-card-value` margin-top | `3px` | `2px` |
| grid gap | `14px` | `10px` |
| card min width | `minmax(220px, …)` | `minmax(165px, …)` |

That takes a card from roughly 90px tall to about 66px. Combined with item 1, the Locations table should start within the first screen at 1440px.

**`19px` is still clearly the headline** against a `10.5px` label and `11.5px` sub-line. Do not go below `18px` — the point of #546 was to make the square footage prominent, and shrinking it too far undoes that.

**Keep the four summary cards (Locations / Total Assets / Services YTD / Spend YTD) slightly larger** than the asset cards — they are portfolio-level, and a small size difference does useful work in the hierarchy.

## 3 · A palette that is actually distinguishable

`MAP_LAYER_DISPLAY` at `storage.ts:5217`. **Randy has approved changing these** — the constraint in #546 is lifted.

| Layer | Now | Proposed | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Irrigation | `#3498db` | **`#3498db`** unchanged | blue reads as water; it is the one that should not move |
| Landscape | `#2E8B57` | **`#2E8B57`** unchanged | sea green reads as turf |
| Trees | `#006400` | **`#7c9a3f`** olive | separates from Landscape while staying plant-like |
| Snow | `#4A90E2` | **`#64748b`** slate | separates from Irrigation; slate reads as winter far better than a second blue |

Four distinct hue families — blue, green, olive, slate — rather than two pairs.

### Two things to check before this ships

**This constant feeds the Portfolio Map**, its rail breakdown, and (after #545) the layer bars. **Take a before/after screenshot of the map** — the legend colours change there too, and that page is the client's opening screen.

**Green against olive is the weakest pair for deuteranopia.** Every card carries its layer label as text, and the bands are now labelled, so nothing depends on colour alone — but if a stronger separation is wanted, Trees moving to a brown or deep amber would be unambiguous at the cost of looking less like foliage. Flag rather than decide.

## What must NOT change

- **Every number on the page.** Layout and colour only.
- **The area-first card content from #546** — ft² headline, count sub-line, coverage caption when partial.
- **The band grouping itself.** Cards stay grouped by layer; only the band's orientation changes.
- **One network request.**
- **CSV export.**
- **`.pfm-*`** — but see the map screenshot note above; the palette is shared.

## How to verify

- [ ] At 2560×1440 the Locations table header is **visible without scrolling**. That is the acceptance test.
- [ ] Each layer band is one row on a wide screen, with its cards flowing across the available width.
- [ ] Below 900px, bands stack as before and nothing overlaps.
- [ ] The four layer colours are tellable apart at a glance in the card grid — check by looking, not by reading the hex.
- [ ] Portfolio Map rail breakdown shows the same four new colours; the map still reads correctly.
- [ ] Square footage headlines are still the most prominent thing on an area card.
- [ ] Summary cards remain visually distinct from asset cards.
- [ ] `grep -rn "006400\|4A90E2" artifacts/` returns nothing — the old values are gone, not shadowed.

## Out of scope

- Changing which metrics appear.
- The Tier 2 table or Tier 3 cards.
- Making cards clickable or filterable.
