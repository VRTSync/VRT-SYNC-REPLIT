# Parity 2 of 4: Rebuild the branch detail Summary tab

*Spec is complete and ready to hand over. Awaiting a task number from Randy — rename to `task-NNN-<slug>.md` once assigned.*

## What & Why

Summary is the first thing a client sees when they open a location, and it is the only screen in the portal with no approved design. Today it renders a KPI strip, a clickable category-breakdown panel, and a two-column Recent Services / Open Work Orders pair. It reads as a list of components rather than a picture of a property.

The approved direction (agreed 2026-08-11) is two rows:

- **Row 1** — the property map on the left, asset count cards on the right. What this property *is*.
- **Row 2** — service metrics, open items, recent activity. How it *is being looked after*.

**Do slice C (layer tabs) after this**, not before. This slice establishes the Summary composition; C makes the per-layer tabs match mockup 04.

---

## ⚠️ Two numbers in the design do not exist in the payload

`getPortfolioBranchDetail` returns `layers`, `inventory`, `recentServices` (latest 20 only), and `openWorkOrders`. It does **not** return:

- a **total service count** — `recentServices` is capped at 20, so a season total cannot be derived from it
- a **photo documentation rate** — computing it from the capped list would be a sample, not a rate

Both must be added to the payload as real aggregates. **Do not derive either from `recentServices`.** A percentage computed off the most recent 20 records is a fabricated statistic that will look authoritative and be wrong.

Add to `getPortfolioBranchDetail`:

- `servicesTotal` — count of completed `task_completions` + completed `service_visits` for this community. Match however the Locations page computes "Services YTD" so the two screens agree; if that is YTD rather than all-time, use the same window and label it the same way.
- `photoProofPct` — completions with at least one photo, over all completions, rounded. Reuse the existing `photoProofPct` SQL from `getPortfolioGroups` or `getAdminDashboard` rather than writing a third version.

If either aggregate is expensive, say so and propose an alternative rather than approximating.

## Row 1 — map and asset cards

**Left, roughly 1.35fr:** the property map, all layers visible, fit to the property. Rendered through `common/map-render.js` like every other map. Zoom, expand, and basemap controls; a small "All layers shown" caption bottom-left. Height around 250–280px — this is orientation, not the working surface. The layer tabs get the tall map in slice C.

**Right, roughly 1fr:** a 2-up grid of asset cards, one per top-level layer category, in the same order as the tabs.

Each card carries three things:

1. Category name
2. Total asset count for the category, large
3. A **sub-line naming the two or three biggest asset types within it**, derived from `inventory` grouped by `layerId` → category

The sub-line is what turns a number into a picture of the property: "39 zones · 2 controllers" rather than just "61". Derive it, do not hardcode type names — `inventory` is data-driven and asset types come from the DB.

Each card has a left border in the category's color (from `layers[].color`, the admin-set value — **do not introduce a second colour source**) and navigates to that category's tab on click.

**Below the cards, full width of the right column:** an unmapped-layer row. Any category with zero assets or no geometry renders as a dashed row reading "{Category} not yet mapped" with a "Request →" affordance. If everything is mapped, the row does not render.

This row is deliberate. It is honest about what is documented, and it is the one place in the product where more mapping is suggested in context rather than as a sales pitch.

## Row 2 — three panels

**Service** — This season (`servicesTotal`), Last service (newest `recentServices` date), Photo proof (`photoProofPct`). Label-left, value-right rows.

**Open items** — one card per open work order: title, reference, estimate, and **Approve / Decline actions inline**. These post to the existing `/api/portfolio/work-orders/:taskId/approve` and `/decline` endpoints. Today Summary shows open items as a count only; a number you cannot act on is just a reminder to go looking. If there are none, render a quiet empty state, not an empty panel.

**Recent activity** — the four or five most recent services: date, title, and a photo-count chip where `photoCount > 0`. Footer link to the Service History tab.

## Done looks like

- Summary is the two rows above and nothing else. The current KPI strip and the category-breakdown panel are gone; the breakdown's job is now done by the asset cards, which also carry the navigation it provided.
- Asset card sub-lines are derived from `inventory`, never hardcoded.
- Category colors come from `layers[].color`.
- `servicesTotal` and `photoProofPct` are real aggregates from new SQL, and `photoProofPct` reuses existing SQL rather than being a third implementation.
- Approve and Decline work from Summary and update the panel without a full page re-render if that is straightforward; a re-render is acceptable if not.
- A location with one mapped layer, or no services, or no open items renders sensibly at every one of those.
- The map is not interactive-heavy: no scroll hijack. Reuse the `interactive: false` preview mode from #490 if that is the right fit, or allow pan/zoom but never scroll-wheel capture — **state which you chose and why**.
- Layer tabs are untouched by this slice.

## Verify

- Willow Creek or the pilot equivalent: asset cards sum to the same total the Locations page shows for that location.
- `servicesTotal` matches the Locations row's Services YTD for the same location. If they disagree, one of them is wrong — resolve before shipping.
- `photoProofPct` is computed over all completions, not the recent 20. Check by counting directly in SQL.
- A category with zero assets appears in the unmapped row and not as a card with 0.
- Approve a work order from Summary → it leaves the open-items panel and the count updates.
- Scroll the page with the cursor over the map → the page scrolls.
- Click each asset card → lands on the matching tab.
- Branch detail layer tabs, Portfolio Map, and the dashboard map preview all behave exactly as before.
