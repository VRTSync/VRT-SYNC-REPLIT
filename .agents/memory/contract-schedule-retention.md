---
name: Contract schedule history retention
description: Approved treatment of removed services and windows with undone completion history.
---

Removing a contract schedule service/window with undone completion history must archive it rather than physically delete the history. Items with no completion history may be deleted. Live completions still block removal until undone.

**Why:** On 2026-11-27 the user approved archival to resolve contradictory requirements: restrictive history references and retained undo rows cannot coexist with physical deletion of their window.

**How to apply:** Preserve completion/photo history, exclude archived items from current schedules, and make active-window resequencing compatible with retained historical windows. Apply the same rule when a whole service is omitted. This is approved scope, not evidence of implementation.
