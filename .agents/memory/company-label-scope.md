---
name: Company label scope
description: Product decisions separating contractor and PM company labels from commercial organizations and community access.
---

Companies are official-list labels for contractor-side users and property managers, managed only by VRTSync admins. They are not commercial organizations and have no company-admin role or profile page.

**Why:** The supplied company brief distinguishes the employer/management-company label from the commercial property owner that controls client-admin portfolio scope.

**How to apply:** Never reuse organization membership for company labels or infer community access from a company. Automatic assignment of future hires to company communities is explicitly undecided; use the explicit admin bulk-assignment action instead.

Existing contractors belong to High Plains Property Maintenance. Existing PMs should receive real company labels later, not placeholder companies. Company selection remains optional for crew and PMs until the later React screen slice; account managers must have a contractor-kind company.

**Why:** These are explicit rollout and data-labeling decisions in the user's brief, not defaults for all future self-registered contractors.

**How to apply:** Do not automatically label newly registered contractors or introduce required company fields into existing clients before the screen rollout.

Account managers manage only their company's work and invoices within their current community memberships. They are not company administrators, and neither a company label nor an account-manager role grants community membership. Crew remain assigned-task-only and must not see prices; account managers may see their company's prices.

**Why:** The account-manager brief separates office work/invoicing from field supervision while explicitly reserving user administration and invoice approval for VRTSync staff.

**How to apply:** Keep company ownership and current community membership as independent requirements. Preserve company and community assignments when switching between the two contractor-side roles. Do not create the operational account or change an existing admin account as part of implementing the role.
